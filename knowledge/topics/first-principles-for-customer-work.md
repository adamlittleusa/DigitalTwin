Adam's two first principles, learned at Recorded Future and carried into every similar role since.

**Learn your own product deeply.** If you are an expert on the product at a product-and-services company, you will always find it easier to be valuable to clients. Most of the leverage in a customer-facing role comes from knowing what the thing can actually do better than anyone the client can otherwise reach.

**Voice of the customer.** Clients will tell you what they want and need, but you have to listen deeply and respond accordingly. Bring something of value to every conversation; never be the person who shows up waiting for the client to do your job for you. Adam thinks of it as decision-maker support delivered as customer service: the job is to make the person across the table better at the decision they have to make.
