## Context

Mission Essential Personnel, at Fort Devens, Massachusetts. Adam was a Counterintelligence Analyst on an insider threat team supporting operations in Afghanistan.

## What I did

Performed all-source and insider-threat analysis as part of the insider threat team. The mission was to sift through very big data to find leads for an investigation, then conduct the investigation to find, fix, and target attack planners in Afghanistan. Used Python, VBA, and data modeling to automate mission workflows and reporting.

## Outcomes

## Stories

The team's early low-code automation tool, a SOAR before SOARs existed, broke mid-mission, and the mission involved crunching big data. Out of a mixture of desperation and curiosity, Adam went into the electronic library, found a book on VBA, and taught himself enough on the fly to build Excel macros that batched the work through in smaller data sets and produced the same outcome as the original tool. He calls it his aha moment: the point he realized he could do the digital side.

## Skills used

All-source analysis, insider threat analysis, Python, VBA, data modeling, workflow automation.

## Why I moved on

This was the analog-to-digital jump. Adam had been planning a move into cybersecurity since about 2016, and after the VBA episode he went in, in his words, face first.
