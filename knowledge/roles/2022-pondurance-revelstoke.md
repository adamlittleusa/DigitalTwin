## Context

One story, two companies. After Recorded Future, Adam took CTI leadership roles to learn what it takes to build these programs operationally, from scratch. Pondurance came first, then Revelstoke. This section is the whole of what the twin says about that period.

## What I did

Pondurance was a growing managed detection and response (MDR) provider that mostly served small and mid-sized businesses and municipal departments, and it had no existing cyber threat intelligence as an input to its services. Adam came onboard to build that out. He onboarded and integrated an intelligence provider, built a homemade threat intelligence platform (TIP) and knowledge base, and set up a daily, weekly, and monthly process for delivering both operational-level and strategic-level reporting to the main MDR SOC function, along with integration of tactical threat feed data directly into the SIEM for detections.

Some key people then left for a unique opportunity at Revelstoke, and they recruited Adam and pulled him along. He worked as a Solution Architect at Revelstoke and also served as an advisor on CTI, up until Revelstoke's acquisition by Arctic Wolf, which dovetailed with the offer he got to move to Accenture.
