## Context

Accenture's Cyber Intelligence group. Adam joined as Deputy Director of Strategy and Services, working remotely. The group had come together largely through acquisitions and was deep in traditional intelligence operations and cyber technical skills. What Adam brought was the commercial CTI vendor side, selling into enterprises, which he had lived at Recorded Future.

## What I did

Built and led a cybersecurity consulting and advisory practice focused on security-program maturity, transformation roadmaps, and enterprise stakeholder engagement. Served as Global Security Delivery Lead for CTI Advisory and as intelligence delivery lead on Secure AI engagements, connecting Engineering, Delivery, Vendor Management, and go-to-market teams. Advised CISOs and security buyers, scoped complex engagements, developed proposals, led delivery, and created go-to-market strategies and MVP components for new security services.

Adam didn't plan to end up at Accenture, but it gave him the chance to build the CTI advisory service he had been arguing for since Recorded Future, directly and from the ground up: the decks and workshop materials, the talk tracks, the delivery design, the pricing, and the initial proposals and statements of work. He sold it within the Accenture ecosystem to a number of marquee brands, Verizon among them, then built a team and managed the delivery.

When he arrived, the group had been selling long-form strategic intelligence and was moving toward a threat-feed product delivered through a managed threat intelligence platform (TIP) partner. Adam argued that the bigger opportunity sat where Accenture traditionally sold work: teaching enterprises how to build better intelligence functions and processes. The group gave him the mandate to build exactly that. The result was a three-tier advisory service that starts with a maturity assessment as its input. Tier one is workshops and gap identification. Tier two adds a roadmap and deeper workshops. Tier three embeds the team to do the work alongside the client and leave better process in place. The service covers building a team and managing intelligence operations, requirements management, analyst skills such as investigations and attribution, TIP management and deployment, insider risk programs, and CTI maturity assessment and benchmarking.

In his deputy role he also helped manage the group's strategic alliances, seeing a significant partnership with Google's Mandiant across the finish line and getting a partnership with Recorded Future up and running.

## Outcomes

- Moved the group from selling a threat feed toward selling advisory; by the time he left, the work had transformed what the department sold and delivered.
- A three-tier CTI advisory service with a maturity assessment at its front, sold to marquee enterprise brands and delivered by a team he built.
- Strategic alliances with Google's Mandiant and with Recorded Future.

## Stories

**From feed seller to advisor.** The group had already decided to move away from long-form strategic intelligence and had landed on selling a threat feed. Adam's argument was that a feed is a product sale, and Accenture's strength is selling the work of making a client better at something. Most CTI teams could buy data; what they lacked was the process and tradecraft to operationalize it, the same gap he had seen from the vendor side at Recorded Future. That argument got him the mandate, and the three-tier service was the answer.

Toward the end of his time at Accenture, Adam did extra work on the side with the Secure AI team, AI threat landscaping, and got what he calls the AI bug.

## Skills used

Practice building, CISO advisory, engagement scoping and proposals, delivery leadership, Secure AI advisory, go-to-market strategy, cross-team coordination, service design and pricing, maturity assessment design, strategic alliance management.

## Why I moved on

He decided he wanted to get smart on AI, work fully AI native, and build AI things and AI security things, because he sees that as a big, big problem. Corelight recruited him to help build their AI story and strategy.
