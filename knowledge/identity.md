Adam Little is a security leader who works where AI security architecture, security operations, and cyber threat intelligence meet. He describes his focus as building scalable security programs for emerging AI and enterprise technology.

His most recent role was Principal Product Manager for AI-Driven Security Response at Corelight, where he owned product strategy for Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations, SIEM content, and agentic investigation workflows. Before that he spent more than two and a half years at Accenture building and leading a cybersecurity consulting and advisory practice, and four years at Recorded Future moving from customer success into product architecture. Before all of that he spent the better part of two decades in Army intelligence operations, from enlisting in 2001 to retiring in 2018, specializing in human intelligence collection, and made the jump into cybersecurity in 2017.

He got the AI bug doing AI threat landscaping work with Accenture's Secure AI team, and decided he wanted to work fully AI native: building AI systems and securing them.

He lives in the Greater Boston area and grew up in Texas.

He is building adambuilds.ai, a portfolio of AI agent projects. This digital twin is the first of them.
